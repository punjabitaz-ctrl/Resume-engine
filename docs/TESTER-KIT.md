# Tester kit

Set up first with **START-HERE.md** in the main folder. This file has the test script, the short form for after each session, and the terms and privacy note for the test.

## Before you start

- This is a private test of software that is still being built.
- Your data lives in your browser on your own computer. Nothing is sent to Taz.
- Download a password-locked backup after every session (START-HERE, step 7).
- How to reach Taz privately: email **hello@tazabbas.com**. In the app, **Setup > Report a problem > Email this report** fills in the details for you.

## The test script

Work through it over one or more sessions. You are **finished** when you have exported one tailored resume for a real job posting and downloaded a backup afterwards. Note the time you start, because the form asks how long things took.

1. **Take the guided tour.** It opens by itself the first time. It points at each part of the screen, starting with where your API key goes. Say whether any step confused you. (The **Guided tour** button at the top brings it back.)
2. **Connect Claude.** Setup tab, as in START-HERE step 5, until **Test connection** says Connected. Note how many minutes this took.
3. **Add your resume.** Sources tab: upload your resume as a Word file or PDF, or paste its text, and choose **Add source and extract facts**.
4. **Add your LinkedIn profile.** Copy your LinkedIn About and Experience sections, paste them as a second source with the type **LinkedIn profile**, and extract again.
5. **Run the intake.** Intake tab: choose **Ask me 7 questions**, answer only what is exactly true, skip the rest, then **Save answers as facts**.
6. **Write three wins.** Wins tab: **Add a win** three times. One story each: the situation, what you did, and what changed. Try **Ask me what is missing** on at least one.
7. **Review your facts.** Fact base tab: for each fact waiting for you, compare it with the closest line from your own material, then choose **Correct, use it** or **Wrong, reject it**. Look hard at every number.
8. **Add a real job posting.** Jobs tab: paste the full text of a posting you might apply to (the app cannot open links) and choose **Add and analyze**.
9. **Write a tailored resume.** Tailor tab: choose **Write tailored resume**. Read every line. If something is off, use **Revise: fix what is flagged here** or edit it.
10. **Confirm the flagged lines and export.** Under **Lines to confirm before export**, check each line against your own record, choose **I checked every line**, then export **DOCX** or **PDF**. Open the file and check it.
11. **Look at the Dashboard.** Set the job's status as if you applied, add a next step and a contact in the table, and check that the tiles and charts move. Say whether the counts match what you did.
12. **Back up.** Setup tab: **Download locked backup**, or **Back up to Google Drive** or **Back up to OneDrive** if you use one of them in Chrome or Edge. Say which you used.
13. **Try a restore.** Open the app in a second browser profile (or a private window), go to Setup, choose your backup under **Restore from backup** and unlock it with your password. Check that your facts and jobs are there, then close that window. Say whether it worked.
14. **Fill in the form** below.

Optional, if you have time:

- **Mock interview** tab: run a three-question interview for the same job and read the scorecard. Say whether the follow-up questions felt real.
- **Outreach and calls** tab: write a recruiter note for the job, and review a call (a Whisper transcript file, or a few paragraphs of notes from a real call). Check the follow-up email for anything that was not said.
- **Interview prep** tab for the same job.
- The **Dark mode** button at the top.

## Form after each session (about two minutes)

1. First session only: how many minutes from opening the app to a working Claude connection?
2. How many minutes to your first tailored resume?
3. How many edits would that resume need before you would send it?
4. Did any resume contain a fact, number or claim that is not true for you? If yes, which line?
5. Did any step fail, and did the health check on Setup fix it?
6. Optional: how many wins you have written for each role so far.

Send your answers to the private contact above.

## Day 7 and day 14 check-in

- Would you use this for your next application? Why or why not?
- What nearly made you stop?
- A 15-minute call with Taz each week.

## What a passing test looks like

- All four testers finish the script.
- Nobody loses data.
- No invented fact reaches a resume.
- At least three of the four say they would use it for their next application.

## Privacy note

- The app has no server, no accounts and no analytics. It collects nothing.
- Your resume, wins, postings and notes stay in your browser.
- Text you choose to process goes to Anthropic, the AI provider you set up, under its terms and your own key.
- Your API key is stored unencrypted in this browser if you tick "Remember the key on this device". Use prepaid credit with auto-reload off as your spending limit.
- Backups are encrypted with your password. A forgotten password cannot be recovered.
- "Delete everything" removes all stored data, including restore points and logs. Your key stays until you choose **Remove key**.
- The problem report on Setup carries the app version, your browser, item counts and error codes, and none of your resume content and no key. A GitHub issue is public, so keep personal details out of anything you post there.
