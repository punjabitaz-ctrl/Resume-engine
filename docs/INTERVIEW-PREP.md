# Interview prep: approach and sources

What the Interview prep tab does and why. Reviewed November 2026. As with the resume standard, rules are marked **firm** where independent sources agree and **judgment** where the evidence is thin.

## What the tab is built on

| Practice | Status | Where it comes from | In the app |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Answer behavioral questions with situation, task, action and result | firm | University career offices (MCPHS, Carnegie Mellon, American University Kogod, Iowa Tippie) and DDI, a hiring-assessment firm | Wins are written in STAR; practice feedback checks which parts were covered |
| Prepare stories that can serve several questions | firm | Kogod advises working on examples usable across competencies | Story bank themes; one win can be matched to several questions |
| Draw likely questions from the job description | firm | Carnegie Mellon notes behavioral questions align closely with the job description | The prep pack is built from the posting |
| Practice out loud and time it | firm | DDI recommends practicing aloud to time answers and fix them in memory; career offices recommend mock interviews | Practice view with a clock, story hidden until asked |
| Prepare questions to ask the interviewer | firm | DDI, Texas A&M career center | "Questions to ask them" in every pack |
| "Tell me about yourself" in a present, past, future shape | firm | Brandeis and Cornell career offices, adapting The Muse | Introduction card with that shape |
| Keep the introduction to roughly 60 to 90 seconds | judgment | Common advice from career sites; no study behind the exact figure | Spoken-length estimate under the answer |
| Aim for about two minutes per story | judgment | Sources vary from "short" to two or three minutes | Guidance text in the practice view |
| Ask who you will meet and how long it will run | firm | It's Up To Us interview guide | Rounds card prompt |
| Know what each stage is for: screen, hiring manager, panel, final | firm | Consistent across career centers and hiring guides | Stage notes on every round and "The whole process" |
| Send a thank-you note to each interviewer within a day | firm on sending, judgment on timing | Texas A&M says 24 to 48 hours, RISD says within 48, others say 24 | Debrief feeds a thank-you draft the user sends |
| Write a debrief straight after | judgment | Standard coaching practice | Debrief fields on every round |

## Limits

- Predicted questions are inferences from the posting. The app says so on the pack.
- The app cannot look up a company. It lists what to research and holds the user's notes.
- Feedback on a typed answer judges structure and specificity. It cannot hear delivery.
- Figures such as "five to seven stories" appear widely but trace to weak sources, so the app shows theme coverage instead of a target count.

## Rules that still apply

- Introduction drafts use only the user's facts, and the app checks their numbers.
- Suggested wording may not add facts or numbers.
- Every draft is accepted only by the user's own tap. Thank-you notes are sent by the user, never by the app.
- No em dashes or en dashes in anything the app writes.
